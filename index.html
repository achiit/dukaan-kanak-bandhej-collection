Hello, I’m Monika Ben

I started Kanak Bandhej Collection in 2022 with a small stock and a big love for bandhej. My mother-in-law taught me how to judge a good knot and a good dye, and I still check every saree that way before it goes on the shelf.

Many of our customers come from Tekra and nearby streets in Deesa. They know they can touch the cloth, ask the price, and take their time. No pressure.
